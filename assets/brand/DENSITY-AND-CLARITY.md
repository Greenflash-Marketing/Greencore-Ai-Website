# Density and clarity

This is the rule marketing Claude underweighted. Greencore pages fail when they feel stuffed, dark-greenflash, or hard to scan. Clean and readable beats flashy and full.

Default field is silver air. Dark is a deliberate beat, not the wallpaper.

---

## Interactive product demo (keep)

Intent: pack an interactive demo of the product on the site. That is the heart of the page, not clutter.

- Demo controls stay (tabs, chips, play, step controls for Simulation / Optimierung / Energiehandel or equivalent).
- The interactive screenshot / live mock is the allowed dense proof object. It can be the one big module in a band.
- Do not surround it with competing card grids, duplicate CTAs, or decorative dashboards in the same view.
- Kill only prototype chrome: "Klickdummy V1.1", Arbeitsstand badges, watermark chips. Not the product demo itself.

Rule of thumb: one interactive demo stage per scroll story. Everything else gets out of its way.

---

## One idea per band

Each vertical band sells one claim. Headline + short lede + one proof object (card, chip module, 50/50, metric row, or the interactive demo). Not headline + three card grids + dashboard + chips + CTA stack in the same scroll window.

If a band needs a legend to explain itself, cut modules until it does not.

---

## Module caps (laptop viewport)

At about 1280-1440px wide, in one screenful of a band:

- Max 2-3 interactive or card modules (the product demo counts as one)
- Max one primary CTA
- Max one dense product surface (interactive demo or dashboard mock). Pair it with air, not another dense grid
- Kickers: one per section title
- No floating prototype badges or persistent "clickdummy / Arbeitsstand" chrome

Below the fold may continue, but do not stack competing systems back to back without a full silver breathing band between them.

---

## Whitespace budget

- Band vertical padding stays in the live range: about `clamp(72px, 10vw, 120px)`. Do not shrink this to fit more modules.
- After a title block, leave clear air before modules (title to lede 16-18px; lede to module at least 32-48px).
- Prefer empty silver over a third card row.
- Short headline measure (about 16ch) and short ledes (about 38ch). Long walls of marketing copy make the page feel packed even when the layout is fine.

Subtract before you add. If the MacBook composition feels busy, remove a module. Do not add smaller chrome.

---

## One card system

All cards share one DNA:

- Radius 12 (cards) / 14 (panels) / 10 (controls)
- Same padding scale, type roles, border language
- Quiet holo hover only (no elevation theater)
- Silver-card on silver, or a clear surface step. Never same-fill-as-background "invisible" cards
- Kunden / testimonials use the same shell as feature cards. Hierarchy comes from content and size, not a new component language

Variants are content slots inside the same shell. Not redesigned chrome per section.

---

## Surface rhythm (clarity, not carnival)

Default page personality = silver + dark type.

- Ultra-dark: sparse beats (one after hero, maybe one CTA band). Not every other section.
- Mid-dark green (`#073D2B`): nested panels and structure. Not full-bleed mood wallpaper.
- Flash: CTAs and voltage moments. Not rare garnish while dark green does the talking.
- Nav: floating, surface-dependent (silver on silver fields). Not permanent dark glass.

Dark to pale to dark on every band reads as Greenflash cosplay and kills calm. Prefer long silver runs with rare dark punctuation.

---

## Understandability test

Before shipping a scroll:

1. Can someone name the section's one idea in five seconds?
2. Is the primary action obvious without hunting?
3. Do cards share one system?
4. Is there unused silver air, or did every gap get filled?
5. Is the interactive demo readable and clearly the focus when present?
6. Remove the word Greencore. Does it still feel unlike Greenflash (cleaner, lighter, less neon-industrial)?

If any answer is no, subtract.

---

## Kill list (density)

- Mid-green as page wallpaper
- Permanent dark-glass nav
- Cards matching the band background
- Kunden cards from a different UI kit
- 4+ competing modules in one view
- Prototype / clickdummy / Arbeitsstand badges (not the product demo)
- Abrupt dark/bright every band
- Filling whitespace because it "looks empty"
- Stripping demo controls "for cleanliness"
