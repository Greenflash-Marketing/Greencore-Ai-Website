## Must / must-not

### Must
- Name **Greencore AI**; DE lead + EN structure
- Software-real surfaces over lifestyle illustration
- Own Greencore skin on shared GF bones
- Zero `#FFFFFF` chrome — silver `#EBF1F5` / `#DCE6ED`
- Dual dark `#00211E`/`#073D2B` + silver bands
- **Flash** `#9FF55D` primary CTA (label `#00211E`)
- **DM Sans + DM Mono** only
- Color ladder: flash primary / soft secondary / lilac tertiary
- Logo: **flash on dark** / **ultra on silver**
- Pointer-reactive gradient **hero type** (SVG/mask quality) — fix, never delete
- Tight headline tracking (**−0.03em**) + leading **~1.05** (see TYPE-AND-SPACE.md)
- **Kickers** = tiny uppercase DM Mono eyebrows above section titles (13px / 0.1em / opacity 0.65) — documented in TYPE-AND-SPACE.md
- Floating inset nav; slim buttons (`10×20`, radius 10); card radius 12 / panel 14
- Subtle holo card hover (light + dark)
- Ultra-dark band after hero
- **50/50:** left solid color + text | right images | 3 synced cases
- **Density / clarity** — one idea per band; max 2–3 modules in view; silver air default; one reusable card system (see `DENSITY-AND-CLARITY.md`)
- motion.dev within budget; `prefers-reduced-motion`

### Must-not
- `#FFFFFF` page chrome; `#009E4F` balanced green
- **ABC Areal** / Areal Mono / Plus Jakarta / third families
- Lilac in logo or as primary CTA
- Soft-flash as logo color
- Full-bleed page/section gradient backgrounds
- Loose headline spacing (wide tracking / tall leading on display)
- Loud GF co-brand; leaf kitsch; elevation-only hover; cluttered heroes
- Overstuffed bands; mid-green wallpaper; dark-glass nav; invisible or reinvented cards; dark↔bright carnival
