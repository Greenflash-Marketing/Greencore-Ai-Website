## Rules
- No lilac in mark or wordmark  
- Clear space ≥ 0.5× mark height  
- Min mark ~24px digital  
- Don’t stretch; don’t recolor off-token  

## Prefer in UI
- Silver nav/footer → `lockup-logo-plus-type-on-silver-ultra.svg`  
- Dark bands → `lockup-logo-plus-type-on-dark-flash.svg`  
- Favicon / app icon → `mark-only-on-dark-flash.svg`
